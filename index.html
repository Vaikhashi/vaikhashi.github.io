    GapBlue Software Labs

    Trainee Associate Consultant, ERP

    I build agentic applications on Oracle Fusion Cloud with Oracle AI Agent Studio, and take them from proof of concept to something a finance or procurement team can actually click through.

    • Multi-branch agent workflows that create, query and navigate Fusion records through REST APIs and business objects.
    • A supplier onboarding validator: document OCR, bank-detail checks, a weighted risk score computed in code nodes, and approve or reject wired straight into Oracle BPM tasks, with an approver dashboard on top.
    • A bank reconciliation loader: Excel uploads validated and loaded through PL/SQL, with a balance check and a reconciliation report.
    • BI Publisher reports with parameterised SQL, such as purchase requisitions traced through to PO conversion.
    • Functional grounding in Procurement and Sourcing: negotiations, supplier responses, awards and purchase orders.

    Favourite bug so far: a query node that returned zero rows with no error and no warning. The fix was one missing limit parameter. I check payload shapes first now.

Face recognition on low-quality images

Matching faces when the photos are blurry, dark or tiny. Built for Jezt Technologies' AI developer assessment.

  • Baseline embeddings from a pretrained InsightFace model.
  • A ResNet18 classifier fine-tuned in PyTorch on the low-quality set.
  • Before-and-after evaluation with ROC, precision-recall and similarity distributions.
